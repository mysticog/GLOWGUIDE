Order matters

Skincare layering guide

Apply products from the thinnest texture to the thickest, so lighter formulas can absorb before heavier ones seal them in.

  1. Cleanser
  2. Toner
  3. Serum
  4. Eye cream
  5. Moisturiser
  6. Sunscreen (morning) or oil (night)

Water-based products go first. Wait about a minute between active serums, and let sunscreen be the very last step in the morning.

Go slowly

How to introduce new ingredients

Skin needs time to adjust. A slow start helps you spot what works, and what does not.

  • Day 1: patch testDab a little on your inner forearm or behind the ear. Wait 24 hours and look for redness or itching.
  • Weeks 1 to 2Use the product twice a week, on non-consecutive nights.
  • Weeks 3 to 4If skin feels calm, move to every other night.
  • Week 5 onwardsBuild to the recommended frequency. Add only one new product at a time.
Avoid the traps

Common routine mistakes

  • Using too many actives at once
  • Exfoliating every day
  • Skipping sunscreen, or applying too little
  • Switching products every week
  • Washing with very hot water
  • Picking at blemishes, which can cause marks
Brand new to skincare?

Beginner skincare routine

Three products are enough to start:

  1. Gentle cleanser morning and night
  2. Moisturiser morning and night
  3. Sunscreen every morning, SPF 30 or higher

Follow this for a few weeks before adding a serum.

The real secret

Skincare consistency tips

Tie it to a habit

Cleanse right after brushing your teeth. Linking steps to something you already do makes them stick.

Keep products visible

Place your essentials by the sink, where you will see them. Out of sight is out of routine.

Judge by weeks, not days

Skin renews over about four weeks. Give a routine at least a month before deciding if it works.

Please note: GlowGuide is educational and does not replace professional advice. Always patch-test new products, and see a dermatologist or doctor for persistent breakouts, rashes, pain, or any change in your skin that worries you.
